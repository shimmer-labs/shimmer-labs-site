The trap

AI is a fake best friend. Every idea you have is gold.

Real email, April 2026, twice

“I came across your Chrome extension and I was impressed by what you’re building. I’ve been working with extension owners to help them get more real users…”

A stranger, selling me users for an app nobody asked for. The Instagram ads say the same thing: pay us, we’ll find your beta testers.
The chatbot never once said “who is this for?”
It’s built to keep you typing. Same playbook as social media.
I’d have spent a couple grand on ads just to find out if anyone cared.
Ten humans at a bar would have told me for free.
Brainstorm with the bot. Validate with a person who’d have to pay.
