My blind spot

Pick the customer who gives you energy.

Do I love fixing my own parents’ Wi-Fi? No. Weird power dynamic. I get impatient. So I assumed I’d hate this.
The lady down the street? Totally different. She’s grateful, she’s got stories, the job’s done in twenty minutes and we talk for forty.
A lot of them live alone. The Wi-Fi was never the whole job. That’s why they text their friends about me.
If your customers drain you, you’ll quit. If they fill you up, you’ll overlook them for a year.
