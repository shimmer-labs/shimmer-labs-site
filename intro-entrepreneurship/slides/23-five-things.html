If you remember five things

What I’d tell freshman me.

1
Talk to people, not just the bot. The bot loves every idea. A stranger who’d have to pay doesn’t.
2
The customer with money and a need probably doesn’t look like you. Go find who’s being overlooked.
3
Run three-month experiments. Customer count or reaction tells you if it’s working. The clock keeps you from changing course every week, and gives you enough data to decide.
4
The hardest part is feeling alone. Find your people. Customers should give you energy, and so should your peers. The Chamber and the coworking space are where I get mine.
5
Relationships are the distribution. Even for a tech business. Especially for a tech business.
