Honest status, September 2026

No business model yet. Just experiments I’m running.

Is it repeatable?

I’ll know when I can say “I have half-days booked.”

First test: a sign-up sheet at one senior community, residents paying per visit. If that fills twice, there’s a pattern. If it doesn’t, I learned something cheap.

Where does it grow?

Wherever my customer already is.

  • Senior living communities
  • At-home care agencies
  • The people already helping them: insurance navigators, the folks who drive them to appointments
  • Referrals from all of the above

Not a TikTok channel. They aren’t there.

What I write down

Every interaction is data.

Who paid, who flinched at the price, who called back, who asked “is this email real?” after the job. Throw it at the wall, read what sticks. That’s the model, for now.

You’ll cover business models and growth later this semester. Watch for the part where the plan has to survive the customer.
