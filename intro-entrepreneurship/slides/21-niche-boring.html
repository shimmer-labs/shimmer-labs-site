What actually pays

The best businesses are niche and boring.

What you want to build

Something cool, new, and radical.

  • An app. For everyone. Especially people your age.
  • Nobody’s done it before, which feels like proof.
  • Hard to explain, harder to sell, hardest to get anyone to pay for.
What actually pays

A small group with a real problem nobody bothers to solve.

  • Narrow: retirees in one town who can’t work their phone.
  • Obvious: everyone knows someone with the problem.
  • Unglamorous: so nobody with a startup hoodie is competing for it.
  • Paid: they hand you cash before you leave.
Boring is a moat. Nobody fights you for the job nobody wants.
