How the pitch got good

“I’m not trying to sell you anything. I’m trying to figure out what I sell.”

What I did
  • Networking through SBU and the Chamber, every week
  • Free work for friends and family. Not a mistake. Research.
  • Said the pitch out loud a hundred times until it stopped sounding like a pitch
  • Taught the AI + Marketing course at Small Business University
What came back
  • Invitations to speak: AI fraud, online safety, the Chamber, the Realtors
  • Credibility, trust, exposure. Conversations started happening to me
  • The elevator pitch wrote itself
  • Customers who want lower costs and more done with less, and they’re the ones paying now
Talk to your customers. Not to AI.
