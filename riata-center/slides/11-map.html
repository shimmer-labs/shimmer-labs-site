How I work

Three questions. Then I build.

1

What do you dread?

They talk, I write. Every task, every annoyance, no filtering. Ten minutes, usually a page and a half.

“What else?”
2

What does the process look like?

Pick the worst one. Walk it step by step on a whiteboard. Where does it start, where does it end, who touches it.

“Then what happens?”
3

What tech does each step touch?

Email, QuickBooks, a spreadsheet, a text thread, a PDF in a folder. That list is the architecture. Now I know what to wire together.

“Where does that live?”
Map the dread, list the tools, and the automation designs itself.
