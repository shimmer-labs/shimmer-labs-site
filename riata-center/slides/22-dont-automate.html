Where I say no

Three things I won’t automate for you

Person-to-person conversations.

The text to a customer whose job went sideways. The reply to a bad review. The “are we still on for Tuesday.” Your voice is the business. Keep it.

The phone during business hours.

A bot answering after hours to triage is fine. Say it’s a bot in the first sentence. Nine to five, a person picks up. People can hear the difference and they hold it against you.

Your social media images.

For the love of Pete. A blurry phone photo of your actual truck, your actual shop, your actual dog beats a perfect AI image every single time right now. Authentic and bad wins. People can tell.

Automate the paperwork. Never the parts that make you you.
