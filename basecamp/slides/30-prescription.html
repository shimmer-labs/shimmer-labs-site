As Prescribed

The prescription.

1

Set a stop time.

Late-night sessions are where scope creep lives. Nothing good gets built at 1 AM.

2

Three humans before you build.

People who would have to pay. Techie Grandkid passes the bar test in one sentence. Shimmer Labs doesn’t. The AI called both great.

3

Hard conversations in your own words.

If it’s personal, read any draft out loud first. Then rewrite it yourself.

4

Ask it to disagree with you.

Then ask again. The second pushback is usually the useful one.

5

New chat, new topic.

Long chats drift. Projects hold the context so you don’t have to.

6

Hands on the wheel.

If you wouldn’t send it, don’t train an AI to send it. Draft, never send.

Use it for the boring parts. Keep the human parts human.
