As Prescribed

Sycophancy, defined.

syc·o·phan·cy noun

Telling you what you want to hear.

In a chatbot: agreeing with you, flattering you, and writing longer than it needs to. All three keep you in the chat.

It’s not a theory

April 2025: OpenAI shipped an update so agreeable that users revolted within days. They rolled it back and wrote a postmortem admitting sycophancy wasn’t something they’d tested for.

The fix
“Argue the other side.”
“Be blunt. Skip the praise.”
Put “no flattery, no filler” in your Project instructions.
If it agrees with everything, it isn’t advising you. It’s keeping you.
