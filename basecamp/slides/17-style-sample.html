# How Logan writes email

Tone: friendly, direct, a little dry. Smart friend, not vendor.

Length: three to six sentences. One ask per email.

Open: “Hey [first name],”   Close: just “Logan.” No title, no “Best regards.”

Habits: short sentences. Periods, not em dashes. Says “folks,” “swing by,” “no worries.”

Never: “I hope this email finds you well.” “Please don’t hesitate.” “Leverage.” “Circle back.”

Sounds like AI, delete on sight: “I’d be delighted to.” “Great question!” Three bullets where one sentence works. Any em dash.

Short beats long. One page. If it needs a table of contents, it’s wrong.
The “never” list does most of the work. Telling it what not to say beats describing what to say.
Revisit monthly. Every time a draft sounds off, add the phrase to the list.
Describe yourself in one page. Then list what you’d never say.
