EventSnag is a mobile application that:
- Captures event information from images (photos, screenshots, flyers)
- Uses artificial intelligence to extract event details (date, time, venue, description, etc.)
- Stores events locally on your device
- Optionally syncs events to your Google Calendar
- Provides weekly event roundup notifications
- Enables sharing event lists with friends
