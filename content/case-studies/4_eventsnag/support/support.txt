## Quick Start Guide

### How to Snag Your First Event

1. **Screenshot an event post** from Instagram or any event flyer
2. **Open Photos app** and find your screenshot
3. **Tap the Share button** (square with arrow)
4. **Select "EventSnag"** from the share menu
5. **Review the extracted details** - our AI does the heavy lifting!
6. **Edit if needed** - AI is smart but not perfect
7. **Add to Calendar** or **Save Locally**

**Pro Tip**: Expand Instagram post descriptions before screenshotting to capture pricing, location, and full details!

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Questions

**Q: What is EventSnag?**
A: EventSnag is an AI-powered iOS app that turns event screenshots into calendar events instantly. Never lose track of events you screenshot from Instagram again.

**Q: How much does EventSnag cost?**
A: EventSnag is free to use with unlimited event capture, AI extraction, and Google Calendar sync. EventSnag Pro ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) offers additional premium features.

**Q: What iOS version do I need?**
A: EventSnag requires iOS 16.6 or later. We recommend iOS 17+ for the best experience.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes! Event data is stored locally on your device. Images are processed temporarily by AI (Anthropic Claude) and not permanently stored. See our [Privacy Policy](https://shimmerlabs.co/case-studies/eventsnag/privacy) for details.

**Q: Can I use EventSnag without Google Calendar?**
A: Absolutely! You can save events locally without connecting Google Calendar. Calendar sync is optional.

### Getting Started

**Q: Why isn't EventSnag showing up in my share menu?**
A: Try these steps:
1. In the share menu, scroll to the bottom and tap "Edit Actions"
2. Look for EventSnag and make sure it's enabled (green toggle)
3. Drag EventSnag higher in the list for easier access
4. If you still don't see it, restart your iPhone

**Q: Can I capture events from the EventSnag camera instead of screenshots?**
A: Yes! Open EventSnag → Tap "Snap a Flyer" to use your camera directly.

**Q: What types of images work best?**
A: EventSnag works with:
- Instagram event posts (screenshots)
- Physical flyers (photos)
- Facebook event images
- Digital posters
- Text-heavy images with clear event details

**Best Results**: Clear text, good lighting, full event details visible (date, time, venue)

### AI Extraction & Accuracy

**Q: Why did the AI get the date wrong?**
A: AI parsing isn't perfect. Common issues:
- Ambiguous dates ("Next Friday" without year)
- Dates in unusual formats
- Multiple dates on one flyer

**Solution**: Always review extracted details before saving. You can edit any field manually.

**Q: What if the AI can't find all the details?**
A: If some fields are empty (like price or age restriction), the AI couldn't find that info in the image. You can:
- Add missing details manually
- Try a clearer photo with expanded text
- Screenshot the full Instagram post with description visible

**Q: Can I re-parse an image if it didn't work the first time?**
A: Currently, no. Delete the event and re-upload the image. We're working on a "Re-Parse" feature!

**Q: Why does the AI keep putting events in the wrong year?**
A: If the flyer doesn't specify a year, AI guesses. We automatically correct obvious errors (like 2023), but cross-year events (Dec→Jan) can be tricky. Always check the year!

**Year Warning**: EventSnag will alert you if the parsed year seems suspicious.

### Google Calendar Integration

**Q: How do I connect Google Calendar?**
A:
1. Open EventSnag → Settings
2. Tap "Connect Google Calendar"
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. Grant calendar access permissions
5. You're all set!

**Q: Can I sync to multiple Google Calendars?**
A: Currently, EventSnag syncs to your primary Google Calendar. Multi-calendar support is planned for a future update.

**Q: Why aren't my events syncing to Google Calendar?**
A: Check these:
1. **Permissions**: Settings → Google Calendar → Make sure EventSnag has access
2. **Internet**: Calendar sync requires an internet connection
3. **Google Account**: Make sure you're signed in (Settings → Google Calendar)
4. **Try Again**: Tap "Add to Calendar" again if sync failed

**Q: If I delete an event in EventSnag, does it delete from Google Calendar?**
A: Yes! Deleting an event in EventSnag removes it from your Google Calendar if it was previously synced.

**Q: Can I disconnect Google Calendar later?**
A: Yes! Settings → Google Calendar → "Disconnect". This revokes access but does NOT delete events already synced to your calendar.

### Notifications & Roundups

**Q: When do I get roundup notifications?**
A: EventSnag sends two weekly roundups:
- **Weekend Roundup**: Thursday at 6:00 PM
- **This Week Roundup**: Monday at 9:00 AM

**Q: How do I turn off notifications?**
A: iOS Settings → Notifications → EventSnag → Toggle off "Allow Notifications"

**Q: I'm not getting notifications. Why?**
A: Check these:
1. **Permissions**: iOS Settings → Notifications → EventSnag → Make sure notifications are enabled
2. **Do Not Disturb**: Check if Do Not Disturb is on
3. **Focus Modes**: Some Focus modes block notifications
4. **Notification Settings in EventSnag**: Make sure you haven't disabled them in-app

**Q: Can I customize notification times?**
A: Not yet! Custom notification times are on our roadmap.

**Q: What happens when I tap a notification?**
A: Tapping a roundup notification opens EventSnag and automatically filters to the relevant roundup (Weekend or This Week).

### Troubleshooting

**Q: The app keeps crashing. What should I do?**
A: Try these steps:
1. **Force Quit**: Swipe up and close EventSnag, then reopen
2. **Update**: App Store → Updates → Update EventSnag
3. **Restart iPhone**: Power off and back on
4. **Reinstall**: Delete EventSnag, reinstall from App Store (note: this deletes locally saved events)
5. **Contact Us**: If crashes persist, email eventsnagsupport@shimmerlabs.co with your iOS version and crash details

**Q: AI parsing is taking forever. What's wrong?**
A: AI processing usually takes 5-15 seconds. If it's stuck:
1. **Check Internet**: AI parsing requires internet connection
2. **Wait a bit longer**: Large images or poor connection can slow things down
3. **Try Again**: If stuck for 60+ seconds, close and reopen the app
4. **Contact Us**: Persistent issues? Email us at eventsnagsupport@shimmerlabs.co

**Q: Google Calendar sync failed. Now what?**
A:
1. **Check Internet**: Calendar sync requires internet
2. **Reconnect**: Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect → Reconnect
3. **Permissions**: Make sure EventSnag has calendar permissions
4. **Google Status**: Check if Google services are down: https://www.google.com/appsstatus
5. **Try Later**: Temporary Google API issues happen; try again in a few minutes

**Q: I can't edit event details. Help!**
A:
1. Make sure you're on the event preview/edit screen
2. Tap the field you want to edit
3. If fields are greyed out, the event may be already saved - delete and re-add
4. Report bugs to eventsnagsupport@shimmerlabs.co

**Q: EventSnag is using a lot of storage. Why?**
A: Event images are stored locally. To free up space:
1. Delete old events you don't need
2. Uninstall and reinstall (warning: deletes all local data)
3. Sync to Google Calendar first if you want to keep events

**Q: I lost all my events after updating iOS. What happened?**
A: iOS updates shouldn't delete data, but if this happens:
1. **Check Google Calendar**: If you synced events, they're safe there
2. **App Groups**: Make sure App Groups are enabled for EventSnag
3. **Contact Us**: Email eventsnagsupport@shimmerlabs.co for help recovering data

## Tips & Tricks

### Get Better AI Results
1. **Expand Instagram descriptions** before screenshotting
2. **Capture the whole flyer** - don't crop important details
3. **Use good lighting** when photographing physical flyers
4. **Avoid blurry images** - AI can't read blurry text
5. **Include visible dates** - "Next Friday" is ambiguous

### Stay Organized
1. **Delete old events** regularly to reduce clutter
2. **Use filters** (Weekend, This Week) to focus on upcoming events
3. **Sync to Google Calendar** for cross-device access
4. **Share roundups** with friends every Thursday for weekend plans

### Power User Tricks
1. **Share Extension is faster** than opening the app
2. **Edit events immediately** after capture to fix AI errors
3. **Set up notifications** to never miss roundups
4. **Disconnect/reconnect Google** if sync acts weird
