## The Problem

You're a Shopify merchant. You know you need email marketing, welcome series, abandoned cart flows, post-purchase sequences. But:

- **Hiring an agency** costs $2,000-10,000 upfront
- **DIY templates** sound nothing like your brand
- **Learning Klaviyo** takes weeks you don't have
- **Writing emails** is torture when you'd rather run your business

So your email list collects dust. Money left on the table. Every. Single. Day.

## FlowMint Changes That

**30 seconds:** Our AI analyzes your store, products, blog posts, brand colors, and voice. It figures out if you're luxury, subscription, consumables, or one of 6 business models.

**90 seconds:** FlowMint generates complete email sequences tailored to YOUR brand. Welcome series. Abandoned cart. Post-purchase thank you. VIP flows. Whatever your business model needs.

**Instant:** Push directly to Klaviyo (or copy/paste to any platform). Your emails are ready to send.

## How It Works

### 1. Brand Analysis
Install the Shopify app and click "Analyze My Brand." Our AI (Claude Sonnet 4) examines:

- **Products**: Names, descriptions, pricing, categories
- **Content**: Blog posts, about page, product copy
- **Visual identity**: Theme colors, fonts, design aesthetic
- **Business model**: Are you premium luxury? Subscription? Consumables? SaaS?

No surveys. No questionnaires. We just read your store like a customer would.

### 2. Flow Recommendations
Based on your business model, FlowMint recommends the 3 most critical email flows. For example:

**Premium Luxury stores get:**
- VIP Exclusive Access
- White Glove Welcome Series
- Personalized Styling Recommendations

**Subscription businesses get:**
- Subscription Renewal Reminders
- Usage Tips & Engagement
- Win-Back for Churned Subscribers

**Standard E-commerce gets:**
- Welcome Series (the classic)
- Abandoned Cart Recovery
- Post-Purchase Thank You

You can also pick from all 18+ flow types if you want to go custom.

### 3. AI Email Generation
Each flow contains 3 emails, carefully sequenced:

1. **First email**: Introduces the topic, matches your brand voice
2. **Second email**: Reinforces the message, adds value
3. **Third email**: Creates urgency or offers incentive

The AI writes subject lines, preheaders, and body copy, all in YOUR voice. No generic "Hi {first_name}!" garbage. Real personality. Real brand alignment.

### 4. Push to Klaviyo (or Export Anywhere)
One click sends your templates to Klaviyo as drafts. They show up in your template library, ready to activate.

Don't use Klaviyo? No problem. Copy/paste works with Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, GetResponse, Customer.io, OmniSend, Shopify Email, or literally any platform that accepts HTML/text.

## Why This Works

### Brand Voice That Actually Sounds Like You
Most AI email tools generate bland, corporate-speak nonsense. FlowMint is different because we analyze YOUR content first.

If your blog posts are casual and funny, your emails will be too. If you write with elegant restraint, we match that. We don't impose a style, we learn yours.

### Business Model Intelligence
A subscription box company needs different emails than a luxury handbag brand. FlowMint knows this. We've mapped 18+ email flow types across 6 business models:

- Premium Luxury
- Standard E-commerce
- Subscription/Membership
- Consumables/Replenishment
- SaaS/Digital Products
- Marketplace/Multi-vendor

You get recommendations that actually make sense for how you sell.

### Speed Without Sacrifice
90 seconds to generate a complete 3-email flow. That's not a typo. Our AI is fast because we've optimized the prompts, parallelized the generation, and cached your brand analysis.

First run takes ~30 seconds for analysis. After that? Regenerate any flow in under 2 minutes.

## Security & Privacy

We take data seriously:
