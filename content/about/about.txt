## The short version

Logan Shimmer is a systems engineer who builds AI-assisted operational workflows for small businesses. Shimmer Labs is the consultancy he runs out of Stillwater, Oklahoma.

The work that became Shimmer Labs was the work Logan was already doing — making complex systems behave, designing the operational glue between tools, shipping reliable infrastructure — before AI became the hammer.

## The career arc

Two decades of systems engineering and operations work, in production, before AI was the category it is now:

- <a href="https://www.ni.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**National Instruments**</a> — test & measurement systems.
- <a href="https://iterable.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Iterable**</a> — marketing infrastructure at scale.
- <a href="https://www.weavegrid.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**WeaveGrid**</a> — clean energy / utility grid platforms.
- <a href="https://sense.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Sense**</a> — clean energy / home energy monitoring.

A deeper interview-led narrative is in progress.

## Teaching and credentials

OSU class of '09 alum. AI for Industry microcredential through <a href="https://ceat.catalog.instructure.com/browse/ai/courses/ai-literacy-and-application-for-technical-professionals" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**OSU CEAT Professional Development**</a> — an applied course on using AI inside technical organizations.

Currently teaching **AI & Marketing** in the <a href="https://www.stillwaterchamber.org/economic-development/small-business-university" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Stillwater Chamber of Commerce's Small Business University**</a> program. Local owners learn what AI can actually do for their shop — without the hype, without getting stuck with one company's product, and without the security mistakes that keep showing up in the headlines.

## Work that came before the brand

The Supabase and <a href="/case-studies/taddy-api-integrations">Taddy</a> projects that anchor our case studies happened **before Shimmer Labs existed.** Same pattern. Same human checking everything before it goes out. Same real, working code in front of real customers. The work didn't change when the brand showed up — that's the proof it was solid all along.

## Why Stillwater

Logan returned to Stillwater after **17 years in California** to build a rooted business at home. Office Hours runs every Tuesday and Thursday, 2-4 PM, at WorkIT Stillwater. Walk-in, free, in person.

## What we do and don't do

We build for owner-operators of service businesses and indie SaaS founders. We don't pretend to be a big-company software vendor, we don't build chat bots, and we don't replace teams. We've been building real software for two decades. AI is a new tool we picked up, not our whole skill set.
