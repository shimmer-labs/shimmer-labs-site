## The short version

Logan Shimmer is a systems engineer who builds AI-assisted operational workflows for small businesses. Shimmer Labs is the consultancy he runs out of Stillwater, Oklahoma.

The work that became Shimmer Labs was the work Logan was already doing — making complex systems behave, designing the operational glue between tools, shipping reliable infrastructure — before AI became the hammer.

## The career arc

About a decade of systems engineering and operations work, in production, before AI was the category it is now:

- <a href="https://www.ni.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**National Instruments**</a> — test & measurement systems.
- <a href="https://iterable.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Iterable**</a> — marketing infrastructure at scale.
- <a href="https://www.weavegrid.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**WeaveGrid**</a> — clean energy / utility grid platforms.
- <a href="https://sense.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Sense**</a> — clean energy / home energy monitoring.

A deeper interview-led narrative is in progress.

## OSU connection

OSU class of '09 alum. AI for Industry microcredential through <a href="https://ceat.catalog.instructure.com/browse/ai/courses/ai-literacy-and-application-for-technical-professionals" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**OSU CEAT Professional Development**</a>.

## Pre-Shimmer client work is the credential

The Supabase and Taddy work that anchors the case studies happened **before Shimmer Labs had its own shingle.** Same pattern. Same human in the loop. Same production-grade work. Pre-shingle isn't a footnote — it's the proof the engineering didn't change when the brand showed up.

## Why Stillwater

Logan returned from California to build a rooted business here. Office Hours runs every Tuesday and Thursday, 2-4 PM, at WorkIT Stillwater. Walk-in, free, in person.

## What we do and don't do

We build for owner-operators of service businesses and indie SaaS founders. We don't pretend to be an enterprise vendor, build chatbots, or replace teams. We bring systems engineering, not just prompt engineering.
