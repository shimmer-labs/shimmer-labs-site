## The short version

Logan Shimmer is a systems engineer who runs the office side of small businesses with their owners, and puts AI on the parts that deserve it. Shimmer Labs is the fractional operations practice he runs out of Stillwater, Oklahoma.

The work that became Shimmer Labs was the work Logan was already doing, making complex systems behave, designing the operational glue between tools, shipping reliable infrastructure, before AI became the hammer.

## The career arc

Two decades of systems engineering and operations work, in production, before AI was the category it is now:

- <a href="https://www.ni.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**National Instruments**</a>, test & measurement systems.
- <a href="https://iterable.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Iterable**</a>, marketing infrastructure at scale.
- <a href="https://www.weavegrid.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**WeaveGrid**</a>, clean energy / utility grid platforms.
- <a href="https://sense.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">**Sense**</a>, clean energy / home energy monitoring.

A deeper interview-led narrative is in progress.

## The other business: Techie Grandkid

Logan also runs <a href="https://techiegrandkid.com" rel="noopener">Techie Grandkid</a>, patient in-home tech help for people in Stillwater who would rather have a person in the room than a chatbot: phones, TVs, email, Wi-Fi, and a free talk called "Don't Get Robbed Online" for the communities and families who need it most. It started because the volunteers at the thrift store where he works kept asking him to look at their phones. Same person, same patience, different customer.

## Work that came before the brand

The Supabase and <a href="/case-studies/taddy-api-integrations">Taddy</a> projects that anchor our case studies happened **before Shimmer Labs existed.** Same pattern. Same human checking everything before it goes out. Same real, working code in front of real customers. The work didn't change when the brand showed up, that's the proof it was solid all along.

## Why Stillwater

Logan returned to Stillwater after **17 years in California** to build a rooted business at home. Office Hours runs every Tuesday and Thursday, 2-3 PM, at WorkIT Stillwater. Walk-in, free, in person.

## What we do and don't do

We run the office side of small businesses with their owners: trades, studios, shops, property managers, and service businesses with 1 to 50 people in Oklahoma. We start with a free Snapshot, scope the fixes in a paid Operations Assessment, and build them together through the AI Concierge, or build and run them with Sidecar when a process is too big to do together. We don't build chatbots, we don't replace teams, and we tell you plainly when AI isn't the answer. We've been building real systems for two decades. AI is a new tool we picked up, not our whole skill set.
