Text:

## We're the local ones

<a href="/about">Logan Shimmer</a> came back to Stillwater after 17 years in California, most of it as a systems engineer at <a href="https://www.ni.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">National Instruments</a>, <a href="https://iterable.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Iterable</a>, <a href="https://www.weavegrid.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">WeaveGrid</a>, and <a href="https://sense.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Sense</a>, making complex systems behave before AI was the hammer. Shimmer Labs is a two-line shop. You talk to Logan directly. No handoffs, no offshore subcontracting, no one between you and the person doing the work.

Most "AI consultants" you'll find on Google are in Dallas, Austin, or a Slack channel six time zones away. If AI is going to touch real customer-facing work in your business, membership changes, quotes, invoices, scheduling, you want the person building it reachable, accountable, and in the room. Here, that's a ten-minute drive.

## Start free: AI office hours at WorkIT

Every Tuesday and Thursday, 2 to 4 PM, at WorkIT Coworking, 901 S Main Street, Suite 86. Walk in, no appointment, no pitch. Bring the one task that eats your week and we'll work on it together at the table. Sometimes the honest answer is "skip it for now," and you'll hear that too. Details on the <a href="/office-hours">office hours page</a>.

## Keep going: the AI Concierge

When you want a standing plan instead of a drop-in, the <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge</a> is two working sessions a month, text access to Logan between them, and a shared hub of everything we automate together. Every task goes through the same three steps: audit how you actually do it, cut the steps that don't need to exist, then automate what's left with you at the keyboard. In Stillwater, sessions happen at your shop when the process lives in the building.

It's priced by team size. $750 a month if it's just you, $1,000 for a crew of 2 to 10, $1,500 for a shop of 11 to 25, $2,250 for a company of 26 to 50. No contract. The next 5 clients get one tier down, locked in. We published <a href="/notes/ai-consultant-cost-per-employee">the per-employee math</a> so you can check our work.

## When it's too big to build together: Sidecar

Some processes need an engineer to build and run them. <a href="/case-studies/sweat-yoga-fitness">Anna Moore at Sweat Yoga & Fitness</a>, across the street from our office, had a booking platform that doesn't let outside software cancel or freeze memberships. We built a <a href="/services/sidecar">Sidecar</a> agent that works the website the way a person would. It handled real customer requests in the first week and ran on its own by week four. Builds from $1,000, half up front and half at go-live, from $250 a month to run.

## Who this is for

Owner-operators in Stillwater, Payne County, and north-central Oklahoma with 1 to 50 people on the team. Trades like plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, and landscaping. Studios, gyms, and salons. Property managers riding the August student-lease wave. Restaurants, shops, and the OSU-connected operators who want a local engineer instead of a vendor. If you're not sure where to start, <a href="/automate-first">we wrote a page per trade</a> on the three tasks worth automating first.

## What we don't do

- Build chatbots for your website.
- Replace your team with AI.
- Sell "set it and forget it" maintenance contracts.
- Pretend to be a big-enterprise vendor with procurement contracts and yearlong audits.

## The fastest way to find out if we can help

Walk in to office hours with the task that's eating your week. If you'd rather start from your desk, put your website into the scanner below and you'll get the three tasks worth automating first, prompts included, in about 20 seconds. And when you want a standing partner, the <a href="/intake">intake form</a> is the front door.
