## Why local matters for AI work

Most "AI consultants" your business will find on Google are in Dallas, Austin, Atlanta, or a Slack channel six time zones away. They charge agency prices, send you to an account manager, and the engineer doing the work has never set foot in Oklahoma.

That works for some businesses. It does not work for most of the ones we work with. If your workflow is going to use AI to handle real customer-facing decisions — membership cancellations, outreach drafts, document parsing, invoicing — you want the person building it to be reachable, accountable, and ideally in the room.

That's the difference. **I do <a href="/office-hours">Office Hours every Tuesday and Thursday, 2-4 PM, at WorkIT in downtown Stillwater</a>.** Walk in, ask whatever you want, no appointment needed, no pitch.
