## Why local matters for AI work

Most "AI consultants" you'll find on Google are in Dallas, Austin, Atlanta, or a Slack channel six time zones away. They charge agency prices, send you to an account manager, and the person doing the actual building has never set foot in Oklahoma.

That works for some businesses. It does not work for most of the ones we work with. If you're going to use AI to handle real customer-facing work — membership cancellations, outreach drafts, invoice parsing, scheduling — you want the person building it to be reachable, accountable, and ideally in the room.

That's the difference. **We hold <a href="/office-hours">free Office Hours every Tuesday and Thursday, 2-4 PM, at WorkIT in downtown Stillwater</a>.** Walk in. Ask whatever you want. No appointment, no pitch.
