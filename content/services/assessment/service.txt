Text:

## First, the free Snapshot

Before anyone pays anything, we talk. That can be <a href="/office-hours">office hours</a> at WorkIT, a 30-minute call, or your website through <a href="/services/concierge#scanner">the scanner</a>. We ask three questions: what do you dread doing, what do you procrastinate, and what would you do with that time back. You leave with a one-page Snapshot: the five tasks eating your week, a rough count of the hours, and the one move we'd make first. It also shows you how we think, so you can decide whether you want more of it.

Most people stop there and go run the prompt. Good. The Snapshot is the free rung on purpose.

## Then the Assessment

The Assessment is for when the Snapshot turned up more than one thing, or the thing it turned up involves more than one person. We spend a day in your business watching the work happen, talk to the people who actually do it, and map how each recurring task really flows: where it starts, who hands it to whom, which tools it touches, and where it breaks. Then we build the plan.

Two weeks after the on-site day you get the written Assessment, in a shared digital copy and a printed one, and we sit down together and walk through it page by page. Nothing in it is a surprise, and nothing in it requires you to hire us again to understand.

## What's in the written plan

- **Where you are.** The business in one paragraph, the team, the tools, and the five tasks from the Snapshot with real hours attached.
- **How the work actually flows.** Three to five process maps, cleaned up from the whiteboard: trigger, steps, handoffs, tools, and the exact spot each one breaks.
- **The hours ledger.** Every recurring task, who does it, hours a week, and what that costs at your loaded wage. One number at the bottom.
- **What to fix and in what order.** Each project with what changes, whether it's a prompt you run yourself, something we build together, or something built and run for you, the tools involved, whether AI is part of it at all, and the hours it gives back.
- **The first 90 days.** Quick wins in the first two weeks, the first real build in month one, and what months two and three look like.
- **What not to automate.** The handoffs that are really conversations, and the steps that should stay human on purpose.
- **What it costs, three ways.** Do it yourself, do it with us, or have it built. With the break-even for each.

## Where AI fits, honestly

Usually in a small slice. Most of what we find is a process with three steps too many, a spreadsheet that should talk to another spreadsheet, and one place where a computer that can read and write would save someone an hour a day. That last piece is where AI earns its keep. The rest is plain operations work, and we say so in the plan. If the answer is "you don't need AI for this," you'll read that sentence in your Assessment.

## What happens after

Three options, and the plan prices all three. Run it yourself with the prompts and the order of operations we give you. Bring it to the <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge</a> and we build it together, month by month, with half the Assessment fee credited to your first month. Or hand the biggest piece to <a href="/services/sidecar">Sidecar</a> and have it built and run for you. Plenty of businesses do some of each.
