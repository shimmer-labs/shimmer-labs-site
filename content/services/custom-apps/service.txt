## Your Idea Deserves to Exist. Let's Build It.

Maybe you've got polished Figma files. Maybe you've got rough sketches. Maybe you've just got a problem you want to solve and no idea where to start.

**All of those work.**

Most agencies will:
- Take 3-6 months (or longer)
- Charge $50k-200k
- Make you talk to account managers instead of developers
- Require you to figure out everything upfront

**We're different:**
- **Meet you where you are:** Complete designs? Great. Napkin sketch? Also great.
- **Anchored speed:** [TreeBidPro](/case-studies/treebidpro) shipped in **2 weeks** from wireframes to production with Stripe live, where agencies typically quote 3-6 months for the same scope. [Paidly](/case-studies/paidly) went from "I have an idea" to a live SaaS processing real payments in **6 weeks**.
- **Direct communication:** You talk to Logan, the developer who built TreeBidPro, Paidly, and EventSnag. No account managers, no offshore subcontracting, no handoffs.
- **Web + iOS:** We build both. Most shops outsource one or the other.

**Real examples with real numbers:**
- **[TreeBidPro](/case-studies/treebidpro):** Wireframes to production with Stripe in 2 weeks. Live, accepting contractor signups.
- **[Paidly](/case-studies/paidly):** Zero to SaaS processing real payments in 6 weeks. 33 paying users, 95% parsing accuracy on production invoices.
- **[EventSnag](/case-studies/eventsnag):** Native iOS app, OpenAI Vision parsing event screenshots into calendar entries at 98% accuracy on blurry inputs. Shipped to the App Store.
