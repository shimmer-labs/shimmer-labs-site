## The Problem

You're a plumber. Or a GC. Or a personal trainer. You're great at what you do —but half your week gets eaten by admin work that has nothing to do with your craft.

Screening job applicants. Posting on social media. Chasing leads. Writing proposals. Answering the same emails over and over.

You can't afford a $40k/year admin assistant. And you shouldn't have to.

## What Sidecar Does

Sidecar is an AI agent that runs 24/7 handling the admin tasks you hate:

- **Hiring** —screens applicants, schedules interviews, handles initial outreach
- **Social media** —creates and schedules posts, manages engagement
- **Prospecting** —finds new customers, generates and follows up on leads
- **Proposals** —drafts project proposals to 80% complete so you just review and send

It's not replacing you. It's the assistant you always needed but could never justify hiring.

## Who It's For

Small service businesses with 1-10 employees:
- Plumbers, electricians, general contractors
- Barbers, nail salons, personal trainers
- House cleaners, landscapers, pet groomers
- Tutors, massage therapists, and more

If you're great at your craft but drowning in paperwork, Sidecar is for you.

## The Math

A $40k/year employee works 2,080 hours per year (40 hrs/week).
Sidecar runs 8,760 hours per year (24/7) at a fraction of the cost.
