## You didn't start your business to do paperwork.

You're a plumber. Or a massage therapist. Or a personal trainer. You got into this because you're great at what you do, not because you love screening job applicants, posting on Instagram, or chasing invoices.

But here you are. Half your week eaten by admin that has nothing to do with your craft. The tasks you procrastinate on pile up. The work you actually love gets squeezed into whatever's left.

## That's what the sidecar is for.

You stay in the driver's seat. You do the work. Everything else, the boring stuff, the repetitive stuff, the stuff you keep meaning to get to, rides in the sidecar.

- **Membership operations**, cancellations, freezes, and modifications against platforms with no API (shipped for Sweat Yoga)
- **Creator outreach**, finds prospects, drafts personalized messages, you approve in Slack before send (shipped for Supabase)
- **Document parsing**, contracts and invoices into structured data with measurable accuracy (shipped on Paidly)
- **Scheduling**, constraint-aware monthly shift drafts for hourly teams (shipped for Sweat Yoga)
- **Lead-to-SMS**, inbound leads triaged and responded to without you watching the inbox

Coming next: hiring, proposals. We build a sidecar for one job at a time. Think of it as a tireless intern. Runs on a schedule, always with you calling the shots.

## Who It's For

Small service businesses with 1-10 employees:
- Plumbers, electricians, general contractors
- Massage therapists, personal trainers, yoga studios
- Barbers, nail salons, house cleaners
- Landscapers, pet groomers, tutors, and more

If you're great at your craft but drowning in the stuff that isn't your craft, Sidecar gives you your time back.

## What would you do with 15 hours a week back?

That's the number we returned to Sarah Gold's creator-outreach desk at Supabase. 5x output, 100+ creators shipped, 15 hours per week returned — over months, not quarters. The right question isn't "how much does an employee cost." It's what *you* would do with that time back.
