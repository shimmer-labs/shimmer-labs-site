Text:

## You don't need another app. You need a partner.

Here's who we keep meeting: a business owner who knows AI could save them hours, tried ChatGPT a few times, and stalled out. Not because they're bad at computers. Because nobody sat down with them and fixed the underlying process first.

Courses don't fix that. You don't have time for a course. Agencies don't fix it either. They disappear into a black box, install something, and leave you with a system you don't understand and can't maintain.

The AI Concierge is the third option: a partner in the seat next to you. Twice a month we get on a working session, you share your screen, and we turn your manual processes into automations together. You watch every step. You understand everything we build. And between calls, you text us when you're stuck.

## How we work: Audit, Optimize, Automate

Every manual task goes through the same three steps, in order:

1. **Audit.** You show us how you actually do it today. Every click.
2. **Optimize.** We cut the fat first. Most 15-step processes only need nine.
3. **Automate.** Then, and only then, we turn the clean process into an automation.

Skipping straight to automate is the classic mistake. Automating a broken process just speeds up the mess. Fixing it first is why this works.

## Priced by team size

One service, four prices, and the price follows how many people you have. A solo operator gets their own hours back. A 30-person shop gets 30 people's hours back from the same two sessions, so it pays more and still gets the lowest per-person price of anything on its books. Count everyone on payroll who touches a computer or a phone for work, regular 1099 help included. We take your word for it on the intake form.

Your rate locks for 12 months. If you grow past your band, we re-tier at your anniversary with 30 days notice. Hiring never costs you mid-year. We did the math on <a href="/notes/ai-consultant-cost-per-employee">what this works out to per employee</a> if you want to check our work.

## How this fits with everything else we do

- **Free AI Office Hours** (Tuesdays and Thursdays at WorkIT): come anytime, ask anything, no charge. The concierge is for when you want guaranteed time, a standing plan, and someone on the hook for your progress.
- **[Sidecar](/services/sidecar)**: we build and run the automation FOR you. The concierge builds it WITH you, so the skills stay in your hands. Plenty of clients do both.

We have room for **6 concierge clients** total, so the service never gets thin. A Company-size client takes two of those seats. When the seats are full, they're full.
