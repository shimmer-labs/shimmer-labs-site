Text:

## Why in person matters for AI work

Some processes can't be fixed over a screen share. The paper on the counter. The whiteboard schedule. The way a job actually moves through your shop, hand to hand. Watching it happen in the building is worth ten discovery calls.

That's the whole idea behind our AI Concierge: two working sessions a month, and when the work calls for it, one of those happens at your Tulsa business. We watch how the work really flows, cut the steps that don't need to exist, and automate what's left, with you at the keyboard so you understand every piece. Between sessions you can text us directly whenever you're stuck.

## What we work on

The same things eating every small business's week:

- Invoices and receipts that a person retypes into QuickBooks
- Estimates and follow-ups that go out late or not at all
- Scheduling that lives in one person's head
- Paperwork between the field and the office

If a task is mostly reading, understanding, and typing, a computer does it better now. Our job is finding those tasks in your business and handing them off, one per session, permanently.

## What we see in Tulsa

Tulsa is about 70 miles and just over an hour east, Highway 51 to the Cimarron Turnpike, and we're in Broken Arrow, Owasso, Jenks, Bixby, and Sand Springs often enough to know which exits back up at four o'clock. The Tulsa-side businesses that reach us tend to be contractors and remodelers juggling draws and change orders, cleaning and restoration companies living in insurance paperwork, event venues and studios booking by text, and lab and medical offices with an onboarding process that is mostly forms. We meet at your business when the process lives there. If it lives on a laptop, video works fine. If you're not sure which task to start with, our pages for <a href="/automate-first/contractors">contractors</a>, <a href="/automate-first/cleaning-companies">cleaning companies</a>, <a href="/automate-first/event-venues">event venues</a>, and <a href="/automate-first/gyms-and-salons">gyms, salons, and studios</a> are the short version of the first session.

## The proof

We do this every week in Stillwater. <a href="/case-studies/stillwater-landscaper">A landscaper's two hours of nightly typing became one photo</a>. <a href="/case-studies/sweat-yoga-fitness">A gym owner's membership paperwork now runs itself while she sleeps</a>. A shop manager's 90-minute monthly schedule became a 5-minute review. Different businesses, same pattern.

## The plain math

The AI Concierge is priced by team size: $750 a month if it's just you, $1,000 for a crew of 2 to 10, $1,500 for a shop of 11 to 25, $2,250 for a company of 26 to 50. Right now the next 5 clients get one tier down, locked in, because we're trying something new. No contracts, cancel anytime. Two working sessions a month plus text access, over video or in person when the process we're fixing lives in the building. If we can't find and kill enough wasted hours to cover that several times over, fire us. The shared progress hub we keep makes that judgment easy: every session, everything built, in one place.

Full details on the <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge page</a>. Same price whether we meet over video or across the counter.
