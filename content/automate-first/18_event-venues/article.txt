## 1. Is That Date Open? Inquiry to Quote

**No more 'is June 14 open?' answered from bed at 11 PM, wrong.**

Where most venues and rental companies start: **Build it together.** Time back: about 4 hrs/week.

What it takes care of:

- Check the date against your calendar before replying, so 'is October 11 open?' gets a yes, a no, or 'that's a peak Saturday, here's how to hold it' instead of sitting until morning
- Answer the same five questions (capacity, alcohol, decor, hours, parking) in your voice, from your real policy sheet, not a 'give us a call' that starts another round
- Ask the two things that change the price before you quote: the date and the head count (or for rentals, the address and what's the ground like)
- Draft the quote from your actual packages and add-ons (chairs, arch, extra hour, generator, delivery fee) and nudge once if it goes quiet after 3 days
- Keep every inquiry (open, quoted, held, booked, passed) in one list, then hand the real conversation to you. The tour stays yours

**Do it yourself this week.** Paste this into ChatGPT or Claude:

> I run an event venue in [city], Oklahoma. Someone is asking if [date] is open for [event type] with [head count] guests. My open dates this month are: [paste open dates]. My policy sheet says: [paste capacity, alcohol, decor, hours, parking rules]. My pricing packages are: [paste packages and add-ons]. Write a reply that answers whether the date is open, answers any of the five common questions they asked, and if the date is booked, offer to hold or suggest alternate dates. Keep it in a warm, direct tone, no exclamation points, sound like a real person texting back, not a form letter.

**Build it together.** Build a shared inquiry tracker connected to your calendar and policy sheet so every question gets a same-day, accurate answer. That is one or two <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge</a> sessions.

**Have it built and run.** Answer date checks and draft quotes the moment an inquiry comes in, day or night, using your real calendar and price sheet. That is <a href="/services/sidecar">Sidecar</a>, built for you and hosted by us.

Works with: Google Calendar, Text, email, and Instagram/Facebook DMs, HoneyBook, Planning Pod, or a Google Sheet.

## 2. Contracts, Deposits, and the Week-Of Checklist

**No more finding out Thursday that the head count grew by 40 and the balance never got paid.**

Where most venues and rental companies start: **Have it built and run.** Time back: about 5 hrs/week.

What it takes care of:

- Send the contract and deposit link the minute they say yes, and tell you when it's signed and paid (and when it isn't, a week later)
- Chase the balance 30 days out, once, politely, so nobody is settling up at the door
- Ask for the final count, the timeline, and the vendor list 10 days out, and flag it to you the day it doesn't come
- Confirm the delivery window and address the day before, and put setup and teardown times on the crew's sheet (for venues: open, vendor load-in, last song, out by)
- When the forecast turns, send your weather policy and the rain plan before they ask, and after the walkthrough, send the damage-deposit note so you're not the one starting that conversation

**Do it yourself this week.** Paste this into ChatGPT or Claude:

> I run a [venue/rental company] in [city], Oklahoma. A client just booked [event] for [date]. Draft the contract-ready message and deposit request I should send now, in my voice, including the deposit amount and due date. Also draft the 30-days-out balance reminder, the 10-days-out request for final head count, timeline, and vendor list, and the day-before delivery confirmation. Use these details: [package, price, event date, address, contact name]. Keep each message short, friendly, and specific, not generic.

**Build it together.** Build a scheduled reminder system tied to your calendar and Square account so deposit, balance, and week-of messages fire on time. That is one or two <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge</a> sessions.

**Have it built and run.** Track every contract and deposit automatically, chase unpaid balances and missing details on schedule, and flag anything overdue the day it happens. That is <a href="/services/sidecar">Sidecar</a>, built for you and hosted by us.

Works with: HoneyBook, Planning Pod, or a Google Sheet, Square for deposits and the balance, Google Calendar, Text and email.

## 3. Photos, Posts, and Review Requests

**No more Sunday night hunting for something to post after you just spent it stacking chairs.**

Where most venues and rental companies start: **Do it yourself this week.** Time back: about 2 hrs/week.

What it takes care of:

- Turn the weekend's phone photos (the arch at golden hour, the slide mid-jump, the tent before the tables went in) into 2-3 posts in your voice, with the couple, the planner, or the photographer tagged once they've said it's fine
- Ask for a Google review the Monday after each event, with the link, in a message that sounds like you
- Draft the seasonal push before the season: spring and fall wedding dates in January, graduation parties in March, Fourth of July and back-to-school in May, so yours goes out first
- Keep a folder of your best shots by season and setup so posting never starts from a blank screen
- Answer the comments and DMs that matter ('do you allow outside catering?', 'what's the price for a Saturday?') and flag any review under four stars the day it lands so you can reply personally

**Do it yourself this week.** Paste this into ChatGPT or Claude:

> I run an event venue or party rental company in [city], Oklahoma. Here's what happened this weekend: [describe setup, event type, standout photo moments]. Write 2-3 Instagram/Facebook captions in a warm, plain voice, no hashtag spam, that could go with these photos. Also draft a short Google review request text to send the couple or client on Monday, with a placeholder for the review link. Keep it sounding like a real person who just spent the weekend setting up and tearing down, not a marketing team.

**Build it together.** Build a seasonal content calendar and photo folder so posts are drafted and ready before the next holiday rush. That is one or two <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge</a> sessions.

**Have it built and run.** Post the season's photos, send the Google review request, and flag any review under four stars automatically, without you touching your phone. That is <a href="/services/sidecar">Sidecar</a>, built for you and hosted by us.

Works with: Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile.
