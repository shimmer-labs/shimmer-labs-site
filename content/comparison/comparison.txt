## TL;DR

**Pick Shimmer Labs Sidecar if** you're running a 1-10 person service business, your back-office work is bleeding hours every week, and you want one engineer who's accountable end-to-end. From $1,000/mo + implementation from $1,000. Live in a week. Cancel anytime.

**Pick something else if** you're an enterprise with procurement, you only need a Zapier between two SaaS tools, you want a chatbot for your website, or your budget caps out below $1,000/mo total.

## Honest comparison

### Sidecar vs. n8n consultants

| | Sidecar (Shimmer Labs) | n8n consultant |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | An engineer who builds the workflow, runs it on your account, and maintains it | Someone who builds an n8n flow and hands you the keys |
| Pricing | $1,000/mo (covers maintenance, model upgrades, edge-case work) | $75-200/hr or flat per flow, no maintenance |
| When it breaks | We see it, we fix it, you find out via a Slack message | You see it, you call them, you wait |
| LLM choice | Claude default, Gemini for high-volume parsing, swap as models evolve | Whatever was current when they built it |
| Best for | Owner-operators who want the engineering AND the operations | Buyers who want to own the flow long-term and have someone in-house to run it |

**They win when** you have an in-house technical person who'll babysit the flow forever. **We win when** you're the in-house technical person and you'd rather not.

### Sidecar vs. Make/Zapier shops

| | Sidecar (Shimmer Labs) | Make/Zapier agency |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Workflows that need real engineering: browser automation, LLM judgment, document parsing, human-in-the-loop approval | Connect-A-to-B integrations between SaaS tools with public APIs |
| When the vendor has no API | We build browser automation that drives the UI like a person would ([Sweat Yoga](/case-studies/sweat-yoga-fitness): WellnessLiving has no membership-action endpoints; we shipped anyway) | They tell you it can't be done |
| LLM-in-the-loop | Native. Claude or Gemini called inside the workflow, with approval gates | Possible but bolted-on, often via OpenAI's $20/mo plugin |
| Pricing | $1,000/mo flat, includes maintenance | $50-150/mo for the Make/Zapier subscription PLUS $1k-5k build fee, no maintenance |

**They win when** the integration is two SaaS tools with clean public APIs and no need for AI judgment. **We win when** the work needs an LLM in the loop, browser automation against an API-less platform, or a human approval gate before anything leaves the building.

**They win when** you need someone who can answer a customer in person, handle exceptions, and use judgment under ambiguity. **We win when** the work is rules-based, repetitive, and the bottleneck is "I keep meaning to get to it."

## Three honest answers

**Where Shimmer Labs is the wrong call.**

- You're an enterprise with procurement. We don't do RFPs, MSAs, or SOC 2 audits.
- Your job is two SaaS tools with public APIs that just need to talk. Hire a Zapier shop for $500. We'll route you to one.
- You want a chatbot on your homepage. We don't build chatbots. We build agents that handle one specific job each.
- Your total budget for back-office automation is under $1,000/mo. We're not the right match yet. Read the [free AI Agents Guide](/ai-agents-guide) and start with DIY.

**Where Shimmer Labs is the right call.**

- You run a 1-10 person service business and 10+ hours of your week is admin work that has nothing to do with your craft.
- You've tried a Zapier or a Make.com flow and watched it break when an edge case showed up.
- You need an LLM in the loop because the work requires judgment (drafting outreach, parsing a non-standard invoice, classifying customer questions).
- The vendor's API doesn't exist for the action you actually need taken.

**Where we won't pretend.**

- We're not the cheapest. A Fiverr freelancer is cheaper to start.
- We're not the biggest. A 50-person agency has more hands.
- We're not the fastest to "sign and ship 50 automations." We ship one workflow at a time and make it work before we build the next.
