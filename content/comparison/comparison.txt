## TL;DR

**Pick Shimmer Labs if** you run an Oklahoma business with 1 to 50 people, the office side still runs through you, and you want someone who fixes the process with you before anyone buys software. Start with a free Snapshot. If there's more than one thing to fix, the Operations Assessment is $1,500 to $2,500 one time. The AI Concierge is $1,000 to $3,000 a month by team size. Sidecar builds and runs the biggest piece for you.

**Pick something else if** you're an enterprise with procurement, you need a full strategic reorganization, you only need a Zapier between two SaaS tools, you want a chatbot on your website, or you're not within a drive of Stillwater and the on-site part matters to you.

## Honest comparison

### Shimmer Labs vs. a fractional COO

| | Shimmer Labs | Fractional COO |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | An operations partner who sits at your counter, maps the work, and builds the fixes with you | An executive who runs operations strategy, hires, and reports to you a few days a month |
| Scope | The recurring work: quotes, invoices, scheduling, follow-up, handoffs, and the software or AI that takes it off your plate | Org design, KPIs, leadership, vendor and hiring decisions |
| Pricing | Assessment $1,500 to $2,500 once; Concierge $1,000 to $3,000 a month | Typically $5,000 to $15,000 a month |
| Team size fit | 1 to 50 | Usually 20 and up |
| Best for | Owners who still do the books and the schedule and want that to stop | Businesses with a management layer that needs running |

**They win when** you have managers to manage and the problem is leadership, not paperwork. **We win when** the problem is Tuesday.

**They win when** the bottleneck is in your head. **We win when** it's in your inbox.

### Shimmer Labs vs. an AI automation agency

| | Shimmer Labs | AI automation agency |
|---|---|---|
| Where it starts | With your process. We cut steps before we automate anything, and we tell you when AI isn't the answer | With the tool. The engagement is scoped as "AI implementation" before anyone has watched the work happen |
| Who does the work | Logan, at your business, with you at the keyboard | An account manager, a strategist, and an offshore build team |

| Best for | Businesses with 1 to 50 people that want to understand what got built | Companies that want it done and don't need to understand it |

**They win when** you have the budget and want to be hands-off entirely. **We win when** the owner needs to own it.

### Shimmer Labs vs. a Zapier or Make shop

| Scope | The whole process, including the parts that aren't software | The connection between two tools that already talk |

**They win when** the job is two SaaS tools with clean APIs. **We win when** the work needs judgment, a website that won't let outsiders connect, or a human in the loop.

### Shimmer Labs vs. a Fiverr freelancer

| | Shimmer Labs | Fiverr freelancer |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | A working system, maintained, with the person who built it on text | A starter workflow you'll debug yourself |

### Shimmer Labs vs. hiring an office manager

| | Shimmer Labs | Office manager |
|---|---|---|
| True cost | Concierge $12,000 to $36,000 a year by team size | A $40,000 hire costs about $67,000 a year loaded |
| What changes | The work gets smaller. The process gets shorter and the boring parts get automated | The work gets moved. Same process, different person doing it |
| Availability | Two sessions a month plus text; automations run around the clock | 9 to 5, when not sick or on vacation |
| Best for | Making the office side small enough that one person can run it | The judgment calls and the customer at the counter |

**They win when** you need a human in the room. **We win when** the goal is to need fewer hours of one. Plenty of clients do both: we shrink the work, then they hire for what's left.

## Three honest answers

**Where Shimmer Labs is the wrong call.**

- You're an enterprise with procurement. We don't do RFPs, MSAs, or SOC 2 audits.
- Your job is two SaaS tools with public APIs that just need to talk. Hire a Zapier shop. We'll route you to one.
- You want a chatbot on your homepage. We don't build chatbots.
- You need a strategic reorganization or a management layer run for you. That's a fractional COO, and we know a couple.

**Where Shimmer Labs is the right call.**

- You run a business with 1 to 50 people and ten or more hours of your week is office work that has nothing to do with your craft.
- More than one thing is broken and you're not sure which to fix first.
- You've bought software, or tried ChatGPT, and it didn't stick because the process underneath was the problem.
- You want to understand and own what gets built.

**Where we won't pretend.**

- We're not the cheapest. A freelancer is cheaper to start.
- We're not the biggest. An agency has more hands.
- We're not remote-first. The on-site day is the point, so we work within a drive of Stillwater.
