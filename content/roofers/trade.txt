## The office work behind every roof

A roofing job isn't done when the last shingle goes on. There's the supplier invoice to get into the books. Job photos to file where you can find them again. The estimate follow-up you meant to send. The insurance paperwork that has to match the scope exactly. None of it is hard. All of it eats your evening.

Here's the pattern: if a task is mostly reading something, understanding it, and writing it down somewhere else, a computer does it better now. That's most of a roofing office.

## What we automate for roofers

- **Supplier invoices.** ABC, SRS, Beacon, whoever you buy from. The invoice hits your email or gets snapped with a phone, and it lands in QuickBooks coded and ready to approve.
- **Estimates and follow-ups.** Measurements and notes turned into a clean quote in your format, and the follow-up that actually goes out three days later without you remembering.
- **Job photos.** Before, during, and after shots filed to the right job automatically, so the one photo you need for a claim isn't lost in a camera roll of 4,000.
- **Insurance paperwork.** Claim documents read and checked against your scope, with mismatches flagged before they cost you a supplement.

## The proof

We've shipped this same shape for other trades in town. <a href="/case-studies/stillwater-landscaper">A Stillwater landscaper</a> turned two hours of nightly typing into one photo of his route sheet. <a href="/case-studies/sweat-yoga-fitness">A local gym owner's</a> membership paperwork now handles itself while she sleeps. Roofing paperwork is the same job: read, understand, type. We just point it at your stack instead.
