## What the fellowship actually is

The <a href="https://watson.is/google-data-center-community-ai-fellowship/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Google Data Center Community AI Fellowship</a> is run by Watson Institute and funded by Google. It runs from September 2026 to January 2027 and is built for people leading local, AI-driven work in the communities where Google operates data centers. Fellows get entrepreneurship and AI training from practitioners, mentorship, financial tools, pitch coaching, and a global cohort across the United States, England, the Netherlands, and Finland.

The part I care most about: every fellow designs and leads a one-day workshop, called a Basecamp, for earlier-stage entrepreneurs in their own community, with a community development stipend behind it. So part of this is a commitment to put on a real, free, in-person AI day for Stillwater business owners.

## Why Stillwater is a Google data center community

Google is building a data center in Stillwater. At the <a href="/notes/stillwater-state-of-the-city-2026">2026 State of the City</a>, the mayor put the numbers at nearly 2,000 construction workers on site today, more than $1.5 million in new sales and use tax already collected, and more than $100 million projected for the city over the next two decades. That's why Stillwater qualifies, and why a fellowship built around data center communities landed on a consultant from a town of 50,000 in Oklahoma.

## What it means if you run a business here

Nothing about how Shimmer Labs works changes. The business is still <a href="/office-hours">free AI office hours</a> every Tuesday and Thursday at WorkIT, the <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge</a> for owners who want a standing partner, and <a href="/services/sidecar">Sidecar</a> when a process needs to be built and run for you.

What the fellowship adds:

- **A free one-day AI workshop for Stillwater entrepreneurs**, the Basecamp, sometime before the program ends in January 2027. Date and venue to come. If you want a seat, come to office hours or <a href="/contact">send a note</a> and I'll put you on the list.
- **The <a href="/ai-security-business">Don't Get Got</a> security material gets a bigger stage.** Small businesses in a boomtown are a target, and that's the piece of the curriculum I'm building out first.
- **Better answers for the questions I get every week.** Part of the program is spending time with people who have done this in other data center towns. I'll write up what transfers and what doesn't, right here in the notes.

## The fine print

Selection for the fellowship is not an endorsement by Google or Watson Institute of Shimmer Labs or its services. It's a training and community program, and I'm in it as a participant. If you want to know what the program is, the <a href="https://watson.is/google-data-center-community-ai-fellowship/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Watson Institute page</a> is the source.
