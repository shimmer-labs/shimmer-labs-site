  question: "What should employees never share with an AI tool?"
  answer: "As a baseline: customer personal data, health or financial records, passwords and API keys, unreleased product or source code, and anything under an NDA or a compliance rule like HIPAA. The guide includes a printable checklist you can hand to your team."

  question: "Is ChatGPT HIPAA compliant?"
  answer: "The standard consumer version is not, and putting protected health information into it can be a violation. There are paths to compliant AI use, including business agreements, approved tools, and de-identifying data, which the guide covers for small clinics and practices."

  question: "Does my small business need an AI policy?"
  answer: "Yes, even a one-page one. Without it, staff make their own calls and sensitive data ends up in places you cannot control. A short, specific policy plus a couple of approved tools beats a vague be-careful every time."
