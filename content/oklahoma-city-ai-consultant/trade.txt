Text:

## Why in person matters for AI work

Some processes can't be fixed over a screen share. The paper on the counter. The whiteboard schedule. The way a job actually moves through your shop, hand to hand. Watching it happen in the building is worth ten discovery calls.

That's the whole idea behind our AI Concierge: two working sessions a month, and when the work calls for it, one of those happens at your Oklahoma City business. We watch how the work really flows, cut the steps that don't need to exist, and automate what's left, with you at the keyboard so you understand every piece. Between sessions you can text us directly whenever you're stuck.

## What we work on

The same things eating every small business's week:

- Invoices and receipts that a person retypes into QuickBooks
- Estimates and follow-ups that go out late or not at all
- Scheduling that lives in one person's head
- Paperwork between the field and the office

If a task is mostly reading, understanding, and typing, a computer does it better now. Our job is finding those tasks in your business and handing them off, one per session, permanently.

## What we see in Oklahoma City

Oklahoma City is about 65 miles and a little over an hour from our office, Highway 51 to I-35, so a morning session at your shop in Edmond, Moore, Norman, Yukon, or Midwest City is a normal Tuesday for us, not a special trip. The businesses that find us from the metro are the same shapes we see at home, just bigger: HVAC and roofing companies whose office can't keep up after a hail week, property managers with hundreds of doors and one inbox, contractors running job costing out of a truck, and small oil and gas operators with month-end living in WolfePak. We meet at your business when the process lives there. If it lives on a laptop, video works fine. If you're not sure which task to start with, our pages for <a href="/automate-first/hvac-companies">HVAC companies</a>, <a href="/automate-first/roofers">roofers</a>, <a href="/automate-first/property-managers">property managers</a>, <a href="/automate-first/contractors">contractors</a>, and <a href="/automate-first/oil-and-gas-operators">oil and gas operators</a> are the short version of the first session.

## The proof

We do this every week in Stillwater. <a href="/case-studies/stillwater-landscaper">A landscaper's two hours of nightly typing became one photo</a>. <a href="/case-studies/sweat-yoga-fitness">A gym owner's membership paperwork now runs itself while she sleeps</a>. A shop manager's 90-minute monthly schedule became a 5-minute review. Different businesses, same pattern.

## Start free, then decide

Everyone starts with a free Snapshot: a 30-minute call, or your website through the scanner below. Three questions, one page back, and you know how we think before you pay for anything.

## The plain math

The AI Concierge is priced by team size: $1,000 a month if it's just you, $1,500 for a crew of 2 to 10, $2,250 for a shop of 11 to 25, $3,000 for a company of 26 to 50. Right now the next 5 clients get one tier down, locked in, because we're trying something new. No contracts, cancel anytime. Two working sessions a month plus text access, over video or in person when the process we're fixing lives in the building. If we can't find and kill enough wasted hours to cover that several times over, fire us. The shared progress hub we keep makes that judgment easy: every session, everything built, in one place.

Full details on the <a href="/services/concierge">AI Concierge page</a>. Same price whether we meet over video or across the counter. If there's more than one thing to fix, the <a href="/services/assessment">Operations Assessment</a> comes first: a day at your business, process maps, and a written 90-day plan, $1,500 for teams up to 10 and $2,500 for 11 to 50, half credited to the Concierge.
