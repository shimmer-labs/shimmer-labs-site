Practical Security
Layer Your Defenses
1
Input Scrubbing
Remove names, IDs, and PII before pasting anything into AI
2
Access Controls
Use institutional tools with proper data agreements, not free-tier
3
Output Review
Verify everything AI generates before sharing or acting on it
4
Monitoring
Track which AI tools are being used and what data flows through them
5
Human Oversight
A human makes the final call — always
No single layer is enough. Stack them.
