Three Levels of AI Engineering
We'll focus on the first two today. The third is still being written.
Prompt Engineering
The buzzword of 2023-24
Instructing AI in a single prompt with the right information to get the output you want. A good prompt has 5 parts.
  • "Write a quiz" gets you garbage
  • "Create 5 MC questions on stress-strain curves for sophomores, 4 choices each, mark correct answer" gets you something usable
  • We'll break down the 5 building blocks next
Context Engineering
The new frontier — 2025+
The supporting info and data AI needs to do its job. Garbage in = garbage out. Trim to the most relevant context.
  • Stripping PII before pasting into ChatGPT
  • Uploading your syllabus so AI knows your course
  • ChatGPT Projects & Claude Projects — persistent memory + docs
Emerging
Intent Engineering
Still being defined — AI agents
The "morals" of the AI — what should the system actually optimize for? Mostly applies to autonomous AI agents.
Klarna replaced 700 support agents with AI. Saved ~$60M. But customer satisfaction dropped 22% — the intent was "resolve fast" not "build relationships." CEO admitted they focused too much on efficiency. Now rehiring humans.
