The Lay of the Land
Quick Vocab Check
LLM
—
Large Language Model. The tech behind ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude.
Generative AI
—
AI that creates new content: text, images, code, video.
Hallucination
—
When AI confidently makes things up. Not a bug — it's how they work.
Training Data
—
The text an LLM studied to learn. Books, code, web pages, chats.
Prompt
—
What you type into the AI. Better prompts = better results.
Prompt Injection
—
Tricking AI into ignoring its safety rules. OWASP #1 LLM risk.
FERPA
—
Federal law protecting student education records. You're bound by it.
On-Prem vs. Cloud
—
Running AI on your own servers vs. someone else's. Security tradeoff.
