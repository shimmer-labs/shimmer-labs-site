Practical Security
The Traffic Light Framework
Go For It
  • Drafting emails or announcements
  • Brainstorming lesson plan ideas
  • Summarizing public information
  • Generating quiz question ideas
Proceed with Caution
  • Anonymized student feedback
  • Aggregate (non-PII) data
  • AI writing on non-confidential docs
  • Creating rubrics or assignments
Don't Do This
  • Student names + grades in ChatGPT
  • AI making final grading decisions
  • Uploading FERPA-protected records
  • SSNs, financial aid, medical records
