Prompt Engineering
Lazy vs. Good Prompt
Lazy Prompt
"Write a quiz"
What you get:
Generic, vague, unusable questions at the wrong level with no answer key. You spend 30 minutes fixing it.
Good Prompt
Role "You are a Material Sciences professor." Task "Create 5 MC questions on stress-strain curves for sophomores." Format "4 choices each, mark correct, add explanation." Constraint "Introductory level."
What you get:
Targeted, level-appropriate, ready-to-use quiz with answer key. Done in 30 seconds.
