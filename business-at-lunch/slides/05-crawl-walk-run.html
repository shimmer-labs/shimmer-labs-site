The Upside · How To Start

Crawl, then walk, then run.

You don’t hand a new employee the keys on day one. Same with AI. Everyone I’m about to show you started small and earned their way up.
Crawl
Ask it
Question and answer, like a smarter Google. You do everything with the answer.
Walk
Automate it
Hand off a repeating task, but a human still reviews before anything goes out.
Run
Trust it
It runs on its own, you spot-check. Rare. And only after it’s earned it.
Go straight to “run” and you’ll get burned. A week of crawling earns the right to walk.
