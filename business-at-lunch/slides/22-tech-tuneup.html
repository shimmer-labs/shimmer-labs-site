How Not To Get Scammed · Technology

The tech side is cheap. Some of it’s free.

1
A password manager. It builds and remembers a strong, different password for every account. You only remember one.
2
Two-factor login on anything with money, customer data, or company secrets. And your email, since it can reset the rest.
3
A backup ransomware can’t reach: offline or a separate cloud. Then test that you can actually restore it.
4
A break-glass account. One emergency login you never use, password sealed in a bank safety-deposit box, for when you’re locked out.
Cheap next to what a scam costs. The money and the reputation. It’s all on the sheet at your table.
