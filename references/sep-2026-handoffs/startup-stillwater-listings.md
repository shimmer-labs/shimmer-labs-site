# Startup Stillwater directory listings (drafted Oct 7 2026)

Site: https://startupstillwater.org (startupstillwater.com redirects there). Built by Kristen Hadley (WorkIT). 66 organizations, 127 "researched resource listings," six categories: Local guides (14), OSU & research (14), Advising & capital (15), Hiring & workforce (6), Markets & community (5), Rules & registration (12). Five "formal partners" (LaunchPoint, Meridian, Riata, Chamber, WorkIT) get a badge; everything else is an "independent resource listing, not a formal partner or endorsement."

Every tile has exactly these fields: location tag (Stillwater / Payne County / Oklahoma / National), category, name, one-sentence description (about 15 to 20 words, noun phrase, no "we"), a count of named resources, "Visit site" (one URL), and an expandable "Browse specific programs" list of named programs (2 to 12 per org). The "Start here" section highlights three doors: Meridian BES, City Development Services, Oklahoma SBDC.

Honest fit note: every current entry is an institution, association, or public program. No private vendors yet. Shimmer Labs fits because the lead resource is free (office hours) and the rest reads as programs. Techie Grandkid is the weaker fit; the angle that works for a founder audience is "the customers you are overlooking" plus "keep your parents from getting robbed while you build," and the free talk.

**Category:** Advising & capital (alternate: Local guides)
**Name:** Shimmer Labs

**Visit site:** https://shimmerlabs.co
**Resources (4):**

1. **Free AI Office Hours.** Every Tuesday and Thursday, 2 to 3 PM, at WorkIT, 901 S Main St. Walk in with the one task eating your week and leave with a plain answer: a prompt you can run yourself, something to build together, or "don't automate that." 4. **AI Concierge.** For the solo founder who is doing sales, invoicing, scheduling, and follow-up alone: a standing operations partner, two working sessions a month, who hands those jobs to automations one at a time so the founder can delegate before there is a team to delegate to. Priced by team size from $1,000 a month. https://shimmerlabs.co/services/concierge

## Tile 2: Techie Grandkid

**Location:** Stillwater
**Category:** Markets & community
**Name:** Techie Grandkid
**Description:** In-home technology help for older Stillwater residents, plus a free fraud-prevention talk for the families, communities, and staff who serve them.
**Visit site:** https://techiegrandkid.com
**Resources (3):**

1. **Don't Get Robbed Online (free talk).** Thirty minutes on how today's scams actually work, three of them shown on a real phone screen, and the one family habit that stops all of them. Free for any Stillwater community, church, club, or front-desk team that will sit down for it. https://techiegrandkid.com/notes/grandparent-scam
2. **In-home tech help.** $80 visits for phones, tablets, email, Wi-Fi, TVs, and "is this text real?" A patient person in the room, not a help line. https://techiegrandkid.com
3. **Help afternoons for communities.** A recurring afternoon at a senior living community or care agency with a resident sign-up sheet, so staff stop being the IT department. https://techiegrandkid.com

**Why it belongs in a founder directory:** Techie Grandkid is also a worked example of what this guide teaches. It started with zero ad spend, one flyer, and a customer group everyone overlooks, and it is the second business of a Stillwater founder who sits in the directory above. Founders who serve older customers, or who have parents they worry about, are the audience.
