## 2. Logan's own LinkedIn post
[VLAD_PROFILE_TAG] had me on his podcast and I said a few things out loud that I'd only ever said in my own head.

The one that stuck with him was my "innies." I run retros with my own AI agents. I tell them what to fix, and they tell me. One of them told me to dial back my output by about ten percent and I'd cut my debugging time in half. It was right. Nobody loves taking notes from software, but it was right.

The one that matters more to me is Anna. She runs a yoga studio here in Stillwater. Twice a year the students move out and she'd get buried in freeze and cancel paperwork, phone in hand at the dinner table. We automated that part. Not the part where she teaches, not the part where she knows her members by name. The paperwork. She got her family dinners back.

That's the whole job, honestly. I automate the things people dread. Never the things that make them human.

Vlad asked what's left for us once the routine work is gone. I think trust, connection, and creativity get more room, not less. You just have to decide what stays human on purpose, before the tools decide for you.
