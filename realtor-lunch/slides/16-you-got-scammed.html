Different scam. Same three moves.

The prince, the fake invoice, the wire on your closing — pull any of them apart and it’s the exact same play.
1 · AUTHORITY

A name, face, or voice you trust — your agent, your title company, your banker, your boss.

2 · THE ASK

Something you’d never normally do — wire funds to a new account, move a closing payment, hand over a login.

3 · URGENCY

“Do it now, before it’s too late.” The rush is on purpose. It’s there to stop you thinking.

See all three lined up? Stop and verify. That pressure was built to manipulate you.
