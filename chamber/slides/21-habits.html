How Not To Get Scammed · People & Policy

Three habits beat a million-dollar firewall.

Have the talk with your team

“The boss will NEVER call or text asking for passwords, account numbers, or gift cards. That business happens face-to-face.” Say it out loud, this week.

Hang up. Call the number you know.

Unknown number claims to be the boss, the bank, or the grandkid? Hang up and call back on the number already in your phone. They control the channel they opened... not the one you open.

Pick a safe word... and a reply

Family and office. Panicked voice needs money? Work the word into a sentence... and they answer with the countersign. Like spies do it. A clone has the voice... not the word.

Urgency is the tell. Slowing down is the defense. No shame in verifying, ever.
