The Upside · Why That Works

Under the hood: a large language model.

Today’s AI is a large language model. It trained on a mountain of writing, so it’s great at three things:
READS
the member’s request in her inbox and the signed paperwork they send back: cancel date, freeze length
WRITES
the reply in her voice, and fills out the contract
UNDERSTANDS
her policy, what a real signature looks like, which buttons to click, and when to flag her for help
Is a job mostly read, understand, or write? Then it’s probably automatable.
