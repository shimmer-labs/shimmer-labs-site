Three lessons I paid for so you don’t have to

From Taddy

Borrow momentum.

Great product, nobody could find it. Zapier and n8n were the marketplace. Your own Shopify store versus the boutique everyone already shops at: start where the customers already are. Their gravity beats your ambition.

From the dream apps

Ten who’d pay is the milestone.

I’d get their app live, then say: find ten people who use it and love it. Not a launch, not an ad budget. Ten. Thirty survey replies saying “cool idea” isn’t validation. Ten saying “I’d pay $40” is.
