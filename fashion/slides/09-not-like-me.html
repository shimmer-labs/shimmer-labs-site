The app I built for me

EventSnag: screenshot a flyer, it lands on your calendar. The customer was someone new to town, like me.
  • I built every feature I wanted
  • Turns out that’s a very small club
  • And it doesn’t have much money
  • Zero paying users

Techie Grandkid’s customer

Retired, offline, frustrated with a phone. Nothing like me.
  • Money. They write checks without blinking.
  • Need. A real, daily, annoying problem.
  • Enthusiasm. So grateful they text their friends about it.
  • Overlooked. Everyone my age says “just make an app.” They don’t use apps.

Your team’s customer

All three proposals target students like you. Run the same four checks.
  • Money. Do they have it, or do they have a Pell Grant?
  • Need. Daily and annoying, or nice to have?
  • Enthusiasm. Would they text a friend about you?
  • Overlooked. Who already serves them? Who serves nobody?
Your customer probably doesn’t look like you. Go find who’s being ignored.
