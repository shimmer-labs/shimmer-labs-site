The habit

The most important person in the room isn’t in the room.

Early Amazon left an empty chair in meetings. So did HubSpot. That’s the customer’s seat. Every decision gets argued in front of it. Yes, even the ones you’d rather not ask about.
Ask the chairWho actually sits here?
Ask the chairWhat do they hate doing?
Ask the chairWhat would they pay to make it stop?
Not a cute ritual. It’s the cheapest research you’ll ever do.
