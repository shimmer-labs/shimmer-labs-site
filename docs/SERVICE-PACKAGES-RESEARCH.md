## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Freelancers
**Their advantage:** Lower hourly rates ($50-100/hour)
**Your advantage:**
- Portfolio of shipped products (Paidly, EventSnag, 4 API integrations)
- Faster delivery (AI agents)
- Better architecture decisions (you've built 3+ SaaS products)

### vs. Agencies
**Their advantage:** Team resources, established brand
**Your advantage:**
- **40-60% cheaper** (no overhead, lean operation)
- **2x faster** (no meetings, no bureaucracy, AI-powered)
- **Restricted stack** (no decision paralysis, expert-level in specific tools)
- **Direct communication** (no account managers, talk to the developer)

### vs. Low-Cost Offshore
**Their advantage:** Ultra-low rates ($15-30/hour)
**Your advantage:**
- US-based, same timezone
- Native English communication
- Higher quality code (TypeScript strict, production-grade)
- Proven portfolio (live products)

## Pricing Philosophy

**Transparency:**
- Show price ranges openly

- Not competing on hourly rate
